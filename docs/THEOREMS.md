## Scope limits

The general minimum-attainment and completion-equivalence theorems do not alone supply a compact closed formula for every nonunitary instance. The rank-one theorem is complete under its defect/invertibility assumptions; it does not silently discharge the phase-admissibility or Cayley-pole conditions.

The S6 centered scalar optimum, global sharpness for `B_cent ≤ 2`, resource certificates and right-angle branch are now included, with physical-image witnesses. Their optimization domain is the complete prescribed rotated transmission family, not an independently proved characterization of the entire BI class at its analytical peak. The general BI peak/task-to-family bridge, phase-global USD certificate in S5, rotated `b=0` family with an additional free transmission phase, and real-algebraic endpoint assertion remain outside the verified scope. The derivation of the assumed GSTCs and numerical plots are not included.

The frame-minimum theorem strengthens the paper verification note's conservative description: the equality to an attained phase-family minimum is proved in Lean. This does not certify the separate explicit finite-candidate evaluation algorithm.
