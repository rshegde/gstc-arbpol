## Scope limits

The general minimum-attainment and completion-equivalence theorems do not alone supply a compact closed formula for every nonunitary instance. The rank-one theorem is complete under its defect/invertibility assumptions; it does not silently discharge the phase-admissibility or Cayley-pole conditions.

The S6 exact resource image and centered Pareto theorem above are now included. The S6 closed-form scalar minimum, its global-sharpness extension when the centered value is at most two, the right-angle noncentered optimum, and the phase-global USD certificate in S5 are **not** claimed as Lean-verified here. The conventional finite-candidate evaluation algorithms and the real-algebraic endpoint assertion also remain outside the verified scope. Neither arbitrary passive-frontier realizability nor derivation of the assumed GSTCs is included. The paper's numerical plots are not part of this proof companion.

The frame-minimum theorem strengthens the paper verification note's conservative description: the equality to an attained phase-family minimum is proved in Lean. This does not certify the separate explicit finite-candidate evaluation algorithm.
